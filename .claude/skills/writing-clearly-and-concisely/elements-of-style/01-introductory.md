# I. Introductory

This book aims to give in brief space the principal requirements of plain
English style. It concentrates attention on a few essentials — the rules of
usage and principles of composition most often violated.

The rules are stated as directly and briefly as possible, without the
qualifications and exceptions a fuller treatment would require. They are a
working discipline, not a complete grammar. A writer who has mastered them can
depart from them knowingly, which is the only kind of departure worth making.
